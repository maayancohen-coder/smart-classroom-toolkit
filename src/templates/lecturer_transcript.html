Record with the browser's microphone. The system keeps only the lecturer's voice (the loudest voice on the microphone) and relevant student questions the lecturer answered. English terms are corrected using a list.

Dictation works in Chrome. The audio is processed by the browser's speech recognition service (in Chrome: Google); the system itself stores no audio, only text. Tell the class before recording. Separating the lecturer from the class is based on microphone loudness, not on speaker recognition, so the lecturer should be the closest to the microphone.
